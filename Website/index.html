About

Our website is a creative tool designed to help graphic design students, beginners, and anyone interested in design create unique posters. The aim of this project is to make the design process easier, more enjoyable, and less stressful, especially when users experience creative block or struggle to find new ideas. Users can create posters by choosing their own colours, fonts, text, textures, and graphic elements. They can also use the random generator to explore different layouts and discover new creative directions. Once they find a design they like, they can make further changes and download their final poster.

About the Plugin

The Interactive Poster Generator plugin is designed to support creativity and save time during the design process. It helps users experiment with different visual elements and generate new poster ideas without having to start from a blank page. The plugin offers a simple way to explore different combinations of colours, typography, textures, and graphics. Users can use the generated designs as inspiration or as a starting point for their own work. Our goal is not to replace creativity, but to support it by giving users new ideas, encouraging experimentation, and helping them turn their inspiration into finished designs.
